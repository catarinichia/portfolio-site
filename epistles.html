Love Letters

01

Into your palms, I lay my swirling head in desperation once more. On a monotonous day, your presence shines upon me like a reward: blissful and heavenly. For deep below my sorrowful heart, I have longed for you in eternity, and in midst of the mundane mayhem, I will continue to seek clarity in your hazel eyes. I speak your beautiful name under my breath every time I yearn for your warm embrace. They become my soothing tune of melody – rather temporarily. Then, when those tunes become the hymn of the crawdads, realization will hit upon you that I, a figure of an imperfect soulmate, have loved you in this particular way. Yet, the crawdads never sung.

Many say that adoration lingers around much longer than our lifetime; and so, I shall adore you beyond my hours of death.
