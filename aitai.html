02 Copy

Today's roundtable brought together the US Consulate and AITAI. At the very heart of St Catherine's College, Stephen Ezell shared how countries are competing on advanced technology through the lens of his experience in The Information Technology and Innovation Foundation.

AITAI Director Jeroen van Dalen hosted a discussion around the rate of AI adoption in Australia and across the world, how different policies in technology and economy take effect, as well as how they impact the job market. With the emergence of AI technology, more ambitious people are adapting to evolving government policies to stay competitive.

We would like to sincerely thank Stephen, Alan Duncan, Cora Lavin, Lisa Longman, Aidan Morgan, Sonny Pham, Kezia Purnomo, Susannah Soon, and Jeroen who contributed their insights and experience to the discussion.
